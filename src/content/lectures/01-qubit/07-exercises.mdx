---
title: "Exercises"
---
1. Verify that $H$ is unitary and that $H^2 = I$. Which rotation of the sphere squares to the identity?
2. Show that $HXH = Z$ by multiplying matrices, and find $HYH$. Confirm both on the sphere.
3. Find the Bloch vector, $\theta$ and $\varphi$ for $|{+i}\rangle$ and for $\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}|0\rangle - \tfrac12|1\rangle$. What are $P(0)$ and $P(+)$ for each?
4. Verify the relations $XY = iZ$ and $\operatorname{tr}(\sigma_j\sigma_k) = 2\delta_{jk}$, and deduce that $(\hat n\cdot\vec\sigma)^2 = I$ for every unit vector $\hat n$.
5. Show that $x = \langle\psi|X|\psi\rangle$, $y = \langle\psi|Y|\psi\rangle$ and $z = \langle\psi|Z|\psi\rangle$, and that $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$ for every unit vector $|\psi\rangle$.
6. Using the series for the exponential and exercise 4, derive $e^{-i\theta\,\hat n\cdot\vec\sigma/2} = \cos\tfrac\theta2\, I - i\sin\tfrac\theta2\,(\hat n\cdot\vec\sigma)$. Check that it is unitary with determinant $1$.
7. Show that $U(\vec n\cdot\vec\sigma)U^\dagger = (R\vec n)\cdot\vec\sigma$ for $U = R_x(\theta)$, where $R$ is the rotation of $\mathbb{R}^3$ about the $x$-axis by $\theta$. Explain why this proves the rotation theorem for that axis.
8. Find $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta$ in the Z-Y decomposition of $H$ and of $X$. Then show that $X R_y(\theta) X = R_y(-\theta)$, starting from $XYX = -Y$.
9. $T^2 = S$ and $S^2 = Z$. Using only $H$ and $T$, can you reach the state $|{-i}\rangle$ from $|0\rangle$? How many gates do you need?
9. You want to estimate $P(0)$ to within $\pm 0.01$ with about $95\%$ confidence. Roughly how many copies of the state do you need?
10. A stochastic matrix $M$ with $M^2 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ does not exist (why?), but a unitary one does. Find a "square root of NOT".
