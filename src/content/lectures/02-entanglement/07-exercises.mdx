---
title: "Exercises"
---
1. Compute $|{+}\rangle \otimes |{-}\rangle$ as a vector in $\mathbb{C}^4$ and check that $\det M = 0$.
2. Show that $(U \otimes V)$ acts on the amplitude matrix by $M \mapsto U M V^{\mathsf T}$, and deduce that local gates preserve $|\det M|$.
3. Write down the other three Bell states $|\Phi^-\rangle, |\Psi^+\rangle, |\Psi^-\rangle$. Starting from $|00\rangle$, which circuits on the simulator prepare each of them?
4. Verify that $(H \otimes H)|\Phi^+\rangle = |\Phi^+\rangle$. More generally, show that $(U \otimes \bar U)|\Phi^+\rangle = |\Phi^+\rangle$ for every unitary $U$.
5. What is the largest possible value of $|\det M|$ for a unit vector, and which states attain it?
6. Prove the teleportation identity by expanding the right-hand side.
7. Three CNOTs with alternating directions swap two qubits. Check this on the simulator, then prove it on basis states.
