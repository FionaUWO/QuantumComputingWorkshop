1. Show that $e^{-iHt}$ is unitary whenever $H$ is Hermitian. Compute $e^{-i\theta Y/2}$ and describe its action on the Bloch sphere.
2. A qubit starts in $|+\rangle$ and evolves under $H = \tfrac\omega2 Z$. Find $P(+)$ as a function of time.
3. Find the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of $H = X + Z$ and confirm the ground energy $-\sqrt2$. Where is the ground state on the Bloch sphere?
4. For the trial states $R_y(\theta)|0\rangle$ and the Hamiltonian $H = Y$, show that $\langle H\rangle = 0$ for all $\theta$ while $E_0 = -1$. What does this say about choosing an ansatz?
5. Write $Z_1 Z_2$ as a $4 \times 4$ matrix. Verify that $\tfrac12(I - Z_1 Z_2)$ is the projector onto the span of $|01\rangle$ and $|10\rangle$.
6. For the path graph on three vertices, list $C(z)$ for all eight strings and write $H_C$ as a diagonal matrix.
7. Show that $H_C$ commutes with $X^{\otimes n}$ and explain what this means for the set of optimal cuts.
8. Compute $e^{-iX\delta}e^{-iZ\delta} - e^{-i(X+Z)\delta}$ to second order in $\delta$ and identify the commutator.
9. Find the ground state and the gap of $H_B = -\sum_j X_j$. Why is it a convenient place to start an adiabatic sweep?
10. Prove that a graph has $C_{\max} = |E|$ if and only if it is bipartite.

<p class="reading">Reading: for exercises 1, 2 and 8, see Nielsen and Chuang, Sections 2.2.2 and 4.7.2.</p>
